Methodology, briefly

Why rolling-origin?

A single train/test split tests one forecast. Sliding the origin forward and re-forecasting at every valid point tests the method, not a lucky split.

Why MASE?

MAPE flatters smooth series and breaks near zero. MASE scales the error against a naive in-sample benchmark computed only from training data, so it is comparable across series and undefined only when genuinely undefined (a constant training window).

Why simple baselines?

Naive, seasonal naive, drift and Holt are transparent, auditable, and fast enough to re-run at every origin. Model complexity is not the contribution here; accountability is.

Why refuse forecasts?

A number is easy to produce. Defending one needs enough independent history to have actually tested the method. When that history doesn't exist, refusing is the honest output.

What does sealing prove?

A SHA-256 hash of the canonical forecast contract, appended to an append-only ledger, proves this exact record existed in this exact form before the outcome was known.

What does sealing NOT prove?

Nothing about forecast quality. A seal can preserve a wrong forecast exactly as faithfully as a right one — that is the point.
